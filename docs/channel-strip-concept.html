02 / Design decisions

One chain. Two ways to work.

Sound survives the mode switch

Simple and Advanced view the same state. Preset recall sets all the controls. Custom saves the entire chain, including order and bypass. Undo makes experimentation recoverable.

Overview + focused detail

The strip keeps signal order and activity visible. Selecting a stage opens its full editor: a spectrum for frequency work, a transfer curve and reduction history for dynamics.

Measurement earns its space

Audio-rate peak capture, timestamped telemetry and background FFT analysis support trustworthy feedback. Hidden views stop expensive drawing while the microphone keeps running.
